# Optional Stitch.ai master design prompt for Loop

Design **Loop**, a premium native-feeling practice journal for iOS and Android. Use the provided `docs/PRODUCT.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md` as source of truth. This is not a budget/finance app, habit-streak app or AI tutor. It helps learners practice guitar, drawing, languages, programming, singing, typing and custom skills, capture their progress with real evidence and compare earlier efforts with new ones.

**Design personality:** 'Calm Momentum': spacious editorial layouts, beautiful typography, purposeful cobalt `#365CF5` on neutral `#F6F7FA` and white cards, nuanced details, unmistakable active states, clean native interactions. Use PesoFlow's restrained organization as inspiration only. Avoid gradient-heavy AI SaaS aesthetics, decorative dashboard analytics, huge generic motivational illustrations, distracting charts, constant gamification, emojis for all navigation, fake user reviews and fake progress scores. Provide light and dark mode consistently.

**User experience:** first skill and session within 60 seconds, without login or permissions. Screens: Welcome, skill category chooser, custom skill setup, Today, Skills list, Skill detail, Goal editor, Exercise editor, Practice setup, Active timer Pause/Resume, Finish Reflection, Session Detail, Progress, skill metric trends, Then vs Now picker, side-by-side image + slider, A/B audio comparison, Library, Search, Reminders, Settings, Storage/Data, Export/Restore preview, App permissions denied, error/empty/long-content variants. The five primary tabs are Today, Skills, Practice, Progress, Library; Settings via header. Practice must be the easiest action.

**Uniquely Loop:** prominently feature real last-session note and next step, a comparison of two user-provided artifacts labeled with dates, meaningful skill-specific metrics with units and sample counts, honest empty states without fake mastery percentages. For audio, show separate A and B players that cannot play simultaneously. For images, show split-view and a before/after scrub affordance. Show human, non-judgmental encouragement: "Practice with intention. See your progress."

**System details:** screen margin 20, 8pt rhythm, card radius 16, consistent line icon set, Inter/system font, distinct hierarchy (30–34 hero, 24–28 screen title, 15–16 body), colors and states from `docs/DESIGN.md`. Support large text and VoiceOver/TalkBack labels. Show useful real product copy and plausible **design-only** sample data; production launch starts empty. Buttons must have named purpose, screen transitions and permission error recovery. Use meaningful imagery where relevant, not generic stock photos.

**Output:** a cohesive all-screen flow with navigation and tokens documented, reusable components and states, light/dark and small/large phone variants, readable layer/component labels. Provide exportable screen source/artifacts and design notes for faithful **Flutter widget** recreation. No real backend or fake integration claims. Report which screens/states are missing, and iterate to address them before calling design complete.
